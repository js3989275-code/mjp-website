MODIFICATION JANTA PARTY
Supporting safe and legal vehicle modification culture in India

🔥 What is MJP?

MJP is a growing modification community for people who love tuned bikes, modified cars, creativity, and automotive culture.

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to support safe and responsible modification culture while building India's biggest enthusiast community.

⚡ Community Values

Respect • Creativity • Passion • Innovation • Safe modifications • Real enthusiast culture.

🔥 Trending MJP Culture

🏍 Bike Modification

“Built with passion. Tuned for the streets. Every modification tells a story.”

“Modification is not just style — it’s personality on two wheels.”

“Ride different. Think bold. Build your dream machine.”

⚡ A New Political Dawn for Modification Culture

• We believe vehicle modification should be safe, creative, and legally respected in India.

• MJP stands for the youth, builders, riders, and creators who want freedom to express their automotive passion.

• Our vision is an India where modified bikes and cars are seen as innovation, not a problem.

• We want responsible modification culture to grow with proper rules, safety standards, and enthusiast rights.

• Modification is not just style — it is identity, creativity, engineering, and passion for millions of youth across India.

🔥 Our 5 Demands

1️⃣ Legal approval system for safe vehicle modifications in India.

2️⃣ Permission for performance exhausts within proper sound limits.

3️⃣ Official modification zones, events, and car/bike meets for enthusiasts.

4️⃣ Recognition of modification culture as automotive creativity and innovation.

5️⃣ Fair rules that support both road safety and enthusiast freedom.
